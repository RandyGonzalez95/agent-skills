# Workspace Cleanup

Inspect actual files, conventions, and task records. Distinguish installed, project-only, exported, and proposed skills. Sidebar organization is not a filesystem move. Historical instructions are evidence, not current authorization.

## Consolidate

Inventory purpose, dependencies, references, output, state, and next action. Read competing versions before merging. Keep one discoverable entrypoint per coherent workflow, preserve discipline boundaries, and move conditional detail into references. Separate changing project facts from reusable methods.

Before removing resources, inspect callers and retain useful scripts, assets, licenses, and attribution. Align lowercase hyphenated names, folders, references, and invocation metadata. Explain any merge, split, or rename in an old-to-new map.

## Preserve and verify

Save useful handoffs before archiving; keep active/blocked work reachable. Record move paths and verify targets remain within authorized scope. Preserve originals or snapshots before replacing untracked collections.

Do not delete session databases or unique records. Cleanup alone does not authorize external publishing, messaging, installation, or schedules.

Validate names, frontmatter, links, references, and affected helpers. Report changes, retained sources, unresolved decisions, and the next usable entrypoint.
