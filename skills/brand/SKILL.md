# Brand

Use the actual business, audience, offer, evidence, and approved assets. Resolve consequential gaps without importing another brand's identity or imposing a style.

## Define the guide

- Positioning: audience problem, offer, substantiated differentiation, and desired action. Separate approved decisions from proposals and explain material tradeoffs.
- Voice: vocabulary, rhythm, formality, and examples grounded in real writing samples.
- Visual identity: color roles and pairings, type hierarchy, spacing, imagery, and logo usage. Label measured and proposed values.
- Assets: approved variants, clear space, minimum size, permitted backgrounds, provenance, and rights. Use the brand's actual rules.

Keep changing prices, account IDs, and campaign decisions in dated project records.

## Review and maintain

Cite each mismatch against the current guide and give a practical correction. Check contrast on actual backgrounds. Preserve editable sources, exports, licenses, and attribution.

Update the established guide and token source only within requested scope. Resolve conflicting decisions instead of accumulating contradictory rules. Deliver a concise guide or prioritized review.

Use graphic-design for asset production, design-system for implementation tokens, and copywriting for finished prose when those tasks are requested.
