# Video Scripts

Establish viewer, takeaway, evidence, duration, and production limits. Use supplied material and voice; future experiments must not read as completed case studies.

## Structure

- Tips: problem, action, adjustment, reason, and credible consequence.
- Completed experiments: situation, friction, test, observed result, and decision.
- Experiment announcements: question, stakes, test, success criterion, and evaluation.
- Personal/founder stories: real moment, tension, choice, consequence, and relevance.

Start with tension, deliver the payoff, and keep one main idea. Do not invent dialogue, emotions, expertise, or client experiences.

For timing, 45-55 words in 20 seconds or 65-100 in 30-45 seconds are starting estimates. Account for pauses and demonstrations; count spoken words and label runtime estimated unless timed aloud.

Separate observed output, judgment, and measured business results. Better drafts do not prove conversion gains, and drafting speed excludes review unless measured. Verify current tool behavior for precise instructions.

## Deliver

Provide an uninterrupted spoken script, with separate aligned shot cues, on-screen text, and captions when useful or requested. For script-only requests, return only the script.

Check evidence, hook/payoff, timing, and shot feasibility. Label illustrative AI visuals. Scriptwriting alone does not run experiments, buy footage, or publish.
