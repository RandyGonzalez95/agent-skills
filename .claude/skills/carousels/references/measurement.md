# Carousel measurement

Record post ID/URL, publication time and timezone, audience, purpose, hook, format, CTA, experiment, production effort, metric source, snapshot time, and post age. Use the source's exact labels for reach, views, impressions, saves, shares, comments, profile actions, and conversions.

Unavailable data stays unknown, not zero. Reach is accounts; views are not interchangeable with reach. Account-wide follower changes are not post-attributed follows. Aggregate metrics do not reveal slide-level retention.

Compare posts of similar purpose at comparable ages. Show raw counts alongside rates. Save or share rate may use accounts reached as a denominator, labeled explicitly; do not calculate when the denominator is absent or zero. Deduplicate resource requests and deliveries by person/request where possible.

Distinguish audience value, relevant conversations, qualified inquiries, and sales. A resource request is not automatically a qualified lead. Use traceable links or campaign parameters where supported, but keep ambiguous attribution unknown.

Recommend one change to test, a primary measure, and an observation window. Assess production effort alongside audience outcomes. Metrics collection, resource delivery, recurring reviews, and publishing are separate operations that need their own requested scope; a content review does not activate them.
